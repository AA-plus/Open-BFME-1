## Compiler experiment and refutation

The unchanged bank reproduces 632 bytes with 35 non-relocation differences at offsets +0xA9 through +0xCC. It copies the source position before loading the player list head. The complete 60-byte callee reached through ILT 0x00001334 only compares x, y and z. It writes no field and returns with ret 4 on both paths. Retail discards its result and subsequently reads the uninitialized distance local before subtracting the member position. The recovery preserves those instructions and does not turn the comparison into a copy.

Making that exact comparison body visible as an inline definition with noinline reduces the target to 11 differences at +0xC1, +0xC2 and +0xC4 through +0xCC. They are one adjacent swap: retail initializes bestTarget before storing sourcePosition.y, whereas the intermediate source emits those stores in the reverse order. Declaring and initializing bestTarget before the position snapshot removes every remaining difference. The helper's emitted 60 bytes independently equal retail with no relocations. Its definition is inline, so it does not add a second strong definition of the already-owned helper.

Typed player views, dword padding, a base-only snapshot, a const base snapshot, /G5, /G6, /Oi, /Op and /Ob1 retain the original 35 differences. A native three-scalar setter gives 36 differences, a const derived snapshot gives 630 bytes with 107 overlapping differences, /G7 gives 631 bytes with 196 overlapping differences, and /Og- gives 965 bytes with 557 overlapping differences. Unsupported __restrict and noalias declarations were rejected by MSVC 7.1. On the 11-byte candidate, delaying the y store gives 29 differences, and moving zero initialization after the node load gives 14. The trial sources and unedited outputs are retained in build/fe270-run/; build/verdict.txt records each measured experiment. Different extents are not treated as acceptable matches.

The final production-path probe is exact over all 632 bytes, with 18 relocation operands. Its raw output is build/fe270-run/production-probe.log. Canonical coordinate and string declarations and canonical global pointee names preserve that result. The old-bank/new-source comparison through name_regression.regressions reports no descriptive-name regression.
