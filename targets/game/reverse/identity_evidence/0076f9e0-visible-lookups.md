## Experiments and refutation

The bank's compiler listing assigns the four numbered lookup key temporaries to one slot, but gives the ternary fallback key a separate four-byte slot. That enlarges the frame from retail's 0x1A0 to 0x1A4 and shifts stack displacements. Complete raw offsets remain in the baseline difference file. After exposing the tree lookup and restoring the frame, the remaining matrix copy interleaves loads with stores, where retail begins with three independent loads at +0x4BC, +0x4BE and +0x4C1. The two remaining empty-fx branches target +0x5D1 rather than retail's +0x5D9.

Canonical NAMEKEY inline overloads and narrowing the formatting buffer to the numbered loop preserve the original 367-byte total mismatch. Replacing the tree's pointer-reference declaration with a signed NameKeyType reference also preserves it. A single explicit shared key local restores the size but swaps the key and weapon-slot stack locations and leaves 117 differences. These hypotheses are rejected by their retained probe and listing files.

Exposing the complete decoded tree find as an inline, non-inlined member restores all target bytes through +0x4BC and reduces the residue to 97 bytes. Those bytes are confined to fallback matrix-copy scheduling and the two empty-fx branch displacements. Exposing the complete decoded out-of-line lookup as an inline, non-inlined member resolves both residues. The compiler can then see that no operation retains the local element's address. No branch spelling, volatile access, assembly or new pin is needed.

The final source uses the canonical Matrix3D and Vector4 declarations. Its `_Construct` specialization is inline and non-inlined, and the visible tree and lookup definitions are inline members. They do not introduce second strong definitions. Separate probes reproduce the existing 71-byte tree, 90-byte lookup, 19-byte `_Construct` and 97-byte value-copy bodies exactly. Raw outputs are build/finish-0076f9e0/helper-tree.log, build/finish-0076f9e0/helper-lookup.log, build/finish-0076f9e0/helper-construct.log and build/finish-0076f9e0/helper-copy.log.

The bank's missing `_STLP_USE_STATIC_LIB` flag also emitted an incompatible 303-byte vector insertion COMDAT with DLL allocator calls. The verified provider's cl line supplies the static-library flag. Adding it leaves the assigned target exact and reproduces the complete 338-byte insertion helper, including the threshold comparison and direct allocation and deallocation calls. The rejected allocator variant and its raw output remain in build/finish-0076f9e0/final-before-static-allocator.cpp and build/finish-0076f9e0/helper-overflow-before-static.log. The successful trial is build/finish-0076f9e0/native-static-allocator.cpp with output build/finish-0076f9e0/helper-overflow-static.log.

The final strict helper verification resolves every relocation and compares the full extents of all six helpers, including the 682-byte range copy and 338-byte insertion helper. Every comparison passes with no masked relocations or unresolved calls. Raw results and selected call targets are in build/finish-0076f9e0/strict-helpers.log.
