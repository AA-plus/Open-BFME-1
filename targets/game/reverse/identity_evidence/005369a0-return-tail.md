## Experiment result and rejected alternatives

The original bank reproduced as 773 bytes with 134 masked differences and 48 missing bytes, for 182 wrong bytes under the repository's screening metric. Its complete raw measurement and strict gate are build/r005369a0-20261013/bank-raw.log and build/r005369a0-20261013/bank-scoped-gate.log. The original difference map is build/r005369a0-20261013/0x005369a0-bytes.json. Four branch displacements at +0x29, +0x2F, +0x3F and +0x6D changed because the epilogues at +0x26A and +0x2DE were cross-jumped into the final tail. The two missing tails explain the subsequent shifted instructions and the 48-byte deficit. The only independent earlier difference was the saved-ESP pair at +0x1A6.

The successful structural hypothesis was a case-local integer assigned on both sides of the text-entry window comparison, with assignment one after the optional text-entry call and assignment zero in the else branch. This definite-assignment form emits all four retail epilogues and their original branch destinations. Initializing the variable to zero before the comparison, using a captured bool, moving the assignment before the call, or returning a constant early produced different code. A conditional expression and a logical expression also reproduced the six-byte candidate, but the explicit integer if/else body is the retained readable form.
