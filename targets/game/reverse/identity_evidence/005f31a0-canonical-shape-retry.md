The exact diagnostic scores and retained object paths are computed in `measurements.json`; these are masked measurements, not acceptance receipts.

The two new declaration experiments therefore provide no byte improvement. The earlier explicit destination, reversed addition and EHsc toggle are not repeated. Reopening requires independently supported helper visibility, pointer lifetime or toolchain evidence that changes the observed SIB encoding, rather than another commuted addition.
