# Preparation body at RVA 0x0021C4B0

The reconstruction is tested against revision e5baa250729b78a33aadc4b41bee11eea2a709ef and the repository's retail 1.03 baseline. Its source is game/GameEngine/Source/GameLogic/Object/Contain/Rva21C710Prepare.cpp. The successful experiment keeps owner X and Y in scalar locals, stores owner Z through a volatile reference to the direction's Z field, copies the selected point with the inline memberwise setter, subtracts the two scalar coordinates, and clears Z. This reproduces the retail floating-point forwarding and the overwritten Z store without assembly.

The original bank emits 485 bytes with 47 differences outside relocation operands. The differences occupy +0xC9 through +0xFE. Retail loads owner X and Y into the x87 stack, forwards point X, subtracts with `fsub st(2)` and `fsub st(1)`, and discards the retained owner coordinates before normalization. The bank first copies point X through its stack home and subtracts directly from object memory. Both versions contain the owner-Z copy and the zero overwrite; earlier records claiming the bank omits that copy do not describe its current bytes. The exact offset list is retained in build/w21c4b0/evidence-checks.log. Raw original and successful probes are build/w21c4b0/bank-full.log, build/w21c4b0/scalar-snapshot-store.log and build/w21c4b0/source-clean.log. The preferred bank was removed only after add_match.py passed its relocation-aware scoped gate; its exact original bytes remain in build/w21c4b0/bank-original.cpp and in the existing immutable attempt history.

## Boundary and receiver

Its complete decoded instructions are retained in build/w21c4b0/decoded.log and its checked call inventory in build/w21c4b0/target-callees.log.

The matched 82-byte dispatcher at RVA 0x0021C710 loads its first stack argument as an object pointer, tests byte +0x90 bit 6, pushes that pointer, and sets ECX to its incoming receiver minus 0x20 before calling ILT 0x0000D468. That thunk jumps directly to this target. The target preserves ECX as its owner, reads the pointer members at +4 and +8, and loads its sole object argument into ESI. No hidden result storage is present. This establishes a member call with one pointer argument, callee cleanup of four bytes, and no consumed result. It does not independently name the owner or the method. The source therefore retains the bank's address-derived owner and descriptive preparation name.

The original `prepare` decoration is contradicted by the ILT oracle. Appending the target address gives `prepare0021C4B0`; the oracle's name tier recognizes the resulting symbol as a placeholder, and the actual ILT guard accepts it. The descriptive word remains intact. build/w21c4b0/late-checks.log retains the original oracle result, the placeholder classification and the guard result. The EA chain label `OpenContain::privateRedeployOccupants` is a lead, not a rename certificate.

The existing bank's global names are preserved and initialized with plain float literals.
