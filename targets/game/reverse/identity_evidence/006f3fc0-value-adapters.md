## New hypothesis and measured progress

The interrupted run had independently justified the three-argument callback pointer and literal floating constants. Its selected baseline is reproduced as build/draw-006f3fc0-resume/baseline.cpp with unedited output in baseline-probe.log. It emits 1,179 bytes with 55 emitted-byte differences and five missing bytes; the original preferred bank emitted 1,179 bytes with 64 differences and five missing bytes. Neither author score was treated as a recovery.

The new hypothesis was that constructing the stride as a one-word value and passing it through a visible inline overload would give its arithmetic and receiver a different front-end lifetime. It would be refuted if the capture setup remained unchanged, acquired an extra call or spill, or lost the decoded argument contract. The value overload fixes the complete capture setup at +0x391 through +0x3AF and the panorama-counter update at +0x3D0 through +0x3DB. The width overload also restores the post-delete reload block. Plain signed and unsigned scalar adapters did not reproduce this improvement.

StrideValue006F3FC0 is a local code-generation utility, not a claim about an EA container or native field type. Its complete constructor writes only the unsigned word at offset zero, and its conversion reads that word. The two overloads forward image and the scalar word to the existing canonical capture and save declarations. Their calls in draw were checked from the actual object relocations: +0x3B0 still names ?captureScreen@W3DDisplay@@IAEXPADI@Z, and +0x3EE still names ?saveScreenShot@W3DDisplay@@IAEXPADII@Z. There are no new adapter call targets in draw. The auxiliary COMDAT definitions are marked absent-from-retail; inlining the call sites does not mean the compiler omits those COMDATs.

The shorter alternative remains build/draw-006f3fc0-resume/prepared-best.cpp with prepared-best-probe.log. It emits 1,179 bytes with 28 differences and five missing bytes. The retained longer alternative has the same raw count of 33 differing or excess/missing bytes, but the repository ranking penalizes a size error twice and therefore prefers its two excess bytes. The selected source and object hashes, offsets, relocation names and ranking calculation are in prepared-preferred-measurement.json. Its unedited full probe is prepared-preferred-probe.log.

## Boundary, identity and ABI

The primary vtable at VA 0x0111EDD0, slot +0x1C, reaches ILT 0x000093EF, which jumps to RVA 0x006F3FC0. The existing W3DDisplay destructor at 0x006EFC20 installs that table at +0x1F of its complete decoded body. Display slot order and ilt_oracle.check with the body RVA confirm ?draw@W3DDisplay@@UAEXXZ. The complete main body has returns at 0x006F4103 and 0x006F445F; its 42 direct branches all land on decoded instructions inside the extent. The target remains eligible according to eligibility.is_dump_row on its current ledger row. No claim or synchronization command was run.

Complete fresh decoded bodies and checked_callees outputs for target, capture, save, render, stats, callback2, callback3, counter-callback, firstview, screenshot and current-debug are retained under build/draw-006f3fc0-resume/ as the corresponding *-decode.log and *-callees.log files. boundary-abi-audit.log lists every return and outgoing branch. The current-debug helper has three outgoing tail jumps; the prior decoded routing evidence remains in build/draw-006f3fc0/current-debug-callees.log. The destructor evidence remains in build/draw-006f3fc0/display-dtor-decode.log and display-dtor-callees.log. The main target has no exception frame, STL value type or constructor cleanup.

Capture reads image and row stride as two 32-bit stack words and returns with ret 8. Its complete pixel loop copies three destination bytes from each four-byte source pixel and advances destination rows by the supplied stride. Save reads image, width and height in order and returns with ret 12. Render uses the ECX receiver and one 32-bit time argument, returns AL at each of its three exits, and cleans four stack bytes. The other three opaque draw helpers consume ECX and no stack arguments. The first-view getter and final screenshot dispatch have no stack arguments; their vtable routes were checked separately. Argument signedness not established by an additive use remains unresolved rather than inferred from a pin.

The inherited captureScreen and saveScreenShot decorations remain contradicted by the ILT oracle. Their decoded contracts and existing bindings were retained, and no naming correction or new pin was invented. The real owner and decoration of draw are confirmed. A recovery would need the inherited helper names resolved or replaced with independently supported address-derived identities; byte equality alone would not settle them. All established bank names and the corresponding original EA comments were retained.

## Remaining mismatch

At +0x3E1, +0x3E7 and +0x3EB the retained candidate loads, multiplies and pushes save width in EAX, while retail uses EDX. At +0x47D the final decrement reads ECX with a six-byte load rather than EAX with a five-byte load. It decrements and stores ECX before loading the vtable; retail loads the vtable in EDX before decrementing and storing EAX. The longer load and store create two excess bytes and shift the last call and epilogue. The earlier exit displacements change with that shifted epilogue.

## Checks and reopening

The strict scoped gates remain red in build/draw-006f3fc0-resume/scoped-short-gate.log and scoped-preferred-gate.log. The actual saved bank was reprobed in bank-final-probe.log and checked again in scoped-bank-gate.log; check_csv-final.log passes after banking. They report the measured byte mismatch and the four unresolved opaque helper bindings at 0x006EB500, 0x006EE800, 0x006F0300 and 0x006F2CC0. The apparent last-call targets in these logs are decoded at shifted candidate relocation positions; decoded retail calls ILT 0x0003809B at +0x493. Those diagnostics do not justify changing the Rva00754A10 pin.

Pin consistency, class-gate and source-name comparison passed in prepared-preferred-pin-consistency.log, prepared-preferred-class-gate.log and prepared-preferred-name-regression-files.log. check_csv passed in prepared-preferred-check-csv.log. The declaration checker refuses the scratch candidate only because it owns zero matched ledger rows, as recorded in prepared-preferred-declared-unmatched.log; the explicit helper annotations themselves are accepted. No game source, ledger, pin, shared header, baseline or policy changed. A full gate was not required for this evidence-only bank update.

Reopening is justified by an independently supported expression or visibility layer that changes the remaining save-width result or the final counter/vtable scheduling while retaining the already matched capture block. The value adapters demonstrate that the earlier register-allocation label was not an exhaustion proof. These experiments do not prove that assembly is required.
