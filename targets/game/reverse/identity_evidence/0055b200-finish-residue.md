## Exact explanation of the residue

Retail keeps the constant one in EBX from +0x301 and materializes its addressable copy at +0x44B. The bank stores it at +0x306. This shifts the intervening instructions by four bytes and exchanges the two local storage groups. The compiler listing puts ladderIndex and lo at EBP-0x3C4, with sideStr, one, and later temporaries at EBP-0x3C0. Retail uses the opposite ownership for those groups, while retaining the unsigned-to-double conversion scratch at its original position.

The differing stack operands occur at retail +0x147, +0x169, +0x223, +0x250, +0x26F, +0x280, +0x447, +0x44F, +0x455, +0x483, +0x492, and +0x52B. The bank's +0x44B instruction corresponds to retail +0x447 because it precedes the point where the streams realign. The two late jump displacements at +0x604 and +0x60B follow from the shifted destinations. The 14 relocation-layout drifts are inside the shifted region; they do not establish wrong callee identities.

An explanatory script moves the compiler object's existing spill, changes those 12 stack displacements, recomputes the existing internal branch displacements, and moves relocation offsets with their owning instructions. It copies no retail instruction bytes and emits no executable. The resulting diagnostic stream has zero remaining masked byte differences. This accounts for the complete residue but does not produce matching C++, prove that assembly is required, or verify relocation identities. Refutation condition: any remaining masked difference after those independent edits disproves this explanation. Raw evidence: `build/qm0055b200/baseline_difference_audit.log`, `build/qm0055b200/baseline.cod`, and `build/qm0055b200/residue_explanation.log`. The reproducible script is `build/qm0055b200/explain_residue.py`.

The retail and compiler unwind maps both contain nine states with the same predecessors. State 4 exposes the same local-slot error: retail destroys sideStr at EBP-0x3C4, while the bank uses EBP-0x3C0. The other eight cleanup receiver offsets agree. This unresolved cleanup displacement is an acceptance blocker even if a future main-body comparison succeeds. Raw evidence: `build/qm0055b200/eh_target.log` and `build/qm0055b200/baseline_object_unwind.log`.
