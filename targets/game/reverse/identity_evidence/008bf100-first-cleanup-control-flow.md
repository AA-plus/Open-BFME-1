# 0x008BF100 first-cleanup control-flow retry

The saved reconstruction remains the best body. It emits 18,655 bytes with 1,080 relocations, 15 positional differences under the probe's relocation mask and one unaligned DIR32 operand. Two new full-body experiments preserve exactly this residue. No source recovery, ledger replacement, identity correction or new pin is claimed.

The tested checkout revision is cad8b7c5926ae06e9ed769454a6af23d3818ef4b. The actual model is gpt-6.1-sol. Trial sources, compiler objects, compiler commands and unedited probe output are retained under build/008bf100-finish/. The saved body targets/game/reverse/attempts/0x008bf100.cpp is unchanged. Its actual probe symbol is ?d_008bf100@@YAXH@Z, although its first-line comment and dump ledger retain ?d_008bf100@@YAXXZ.

## Remaining instructions

Retail stores the first destination at +0x9A, writes the exception state at +0x9F using [esp+0x2D8], decrements the reference word at +0xA6, compares it with zero at +0xA9 and pushes EDI at +0xAD. The bank pushes EDI at +0x9A, then performs those four operations at +0x9B, +0xA0, +0xA7 and +0xAA. Its state store uses [esp+0x2DC], accounting for the earlier push. Both streams resume the same branch at +0xAE. EDI's first working use is the allocation/zeroing tail at +0x48AB.

The probe reports differences at +0x9A, +0x9B, +0xA0, +0xA1, +0xA2, +0xA3, +0xA4, +0xA6, +0xA7, +0xA8, +0xA9, +0xAA, +0xAB, +0xAC and +0xAD. The destination-store operand starts at retail +0x9B and compiled +0x9C. The positional difference count includes the effects of this misaligned relocation mask; it is a diagnostic ranking measure, not an acceptance certificate. The complete per-byte values and compile command are in build/008bf100-finish/baseline-measurement.json. The raw full-body comparison is build/008bf100-finish/baseline-probe.log.

## New hypothesis and rejected trials

The hypothesis was that a control-flow boundary around the first full-expression cleanup could change MSVC's delayed EDI save placement without changing ownership, the other temporary lifetimes or the allocation tail. The supporting observation was that the mismatch begins at this first cleanup, while the complete remainder rejoins and matches structurally. The refutation condition was preservation of the same save/store/state/decrement/comparison sequence in both new control-flow forms.

An explicit goto immediately after the first assignment, with its destination at the next case, emitted the same 18,655 bytes, 15 positional differences and one unaligned relocation. A one-iteration for loop around the first assignment emitted the same result. Both sources retain the original declarations and names. The sources and raw outputs are build/008bf100-finish/first-cleanup-goto.cpp, build/008bf100-finish/first-cleanup-goto-probe.log, build/008bf100-finish/first-cleanup-loop.cpp and build/008bf100-finish/first-cleanup-loop-probe.log. Their measurement JSON files also retain the exact offsets and compiler commands. The trial alternatives were generated through shape_search.variants from build/008bf100-finish/first-cleanup-choices.json, and each trial checked STOP_WORKER before compilation.

The full nine-row attempt history and the saved continuation index were read before these trials. The EH generator reproduced only the already-exhausted setter throw() and /EHsc- alternatives, so those were excluded. The register, copy, store and frame generator produced only an alias in the unused copy constructor; it cannot affect the target's emitted path and was excluded. The generated choices remain in build/008bf100-finish/generated-eh.json and build/008bf100-finish/generated-families.json. Previously recorded release/barrier rewrites, typed table declarations, local scopes, first-assignment constant switch, setter visibility and compiler-option variants were not repeated. After the two unchanged experiments, this retry has no justified next experiment.

## Boundary, ABI and type evidence

The complete retail target, caller 0x00894800 (524 bytes), setter 0x0089E680 (125 bytes) and destructor 0x00891B80 (22 bytes) were decoded with branch destinations checked on instruction boundaries. The target has one normal RET at +0x48DE, followed by INT3 and the independently claimed next body. Its conditional branches remain within the extent. The checked-callee output reports 178 direct calls, all to the same setter, and no outgoing direct jump. Full decodes and checked-callee logs are build/008bf100-finish/decoded-008bf100.log, build/008bf100-finish/decoded-00894800.log, build/008bf100-finish/decoded-0089e680.log, build/008bf100-finish/decoded-00891b80.log and the corresponding checked logs.

The caller loads a four-byte configuration field at receiver +0x38, pushes it at 0x008948F1 and calls the target at 0x008948F6. It removes this argument together with the subsequent allocator argument at 0x00894908. The target consumes that four-byte value, uses a plain RET and exposes no used return or hidden return storage. This supports the bank's cdecl void(int) ABI view. It does not establish the original function name or the signedness of the configuration declaration.

The complete setter consumes ECX as a four-byte handle receiver and one C-string pointer, returning with RET 4 on both paths. Its nonempty path writes WORD reference count at header +0, WORD length at +2, WORD capacity at +4 and a zero WORD at +6, then copies the terminated character payload at +8. Its empty path stores the shared empty block and increments that block's reference word. The complete destructor loads the handle at receiver +0, decrements the same WORD and conditionally frees the entire header pointer. These accesses establish the handle and header widths independently of allocation size or a donor template. The bank's existing m_flags spelling for +6 is retained; this retry does not establish that word's semantic name. No STL value-type inference is involved, and the hash-node payload remains opaque.

The declarations in the bank were compared with BfmeConv1402.cpp, EAStringCMid.cpp and Apt.cpp. No shared header for BfmeStrVKI was found in the relevant include trees. Those sources confirm the local handle/header view and the two-slot allocation pair; they do not identify the registry function. The existing EAStringC destructor identity at 0x00891B80 is supported by targets/game/reverse/identity_evidence/00891b80-eastring-return-cleanup.md. No bank name was changed.

## Exception cleanup and indirect callbacks

Retail has 178 unwind states, each with predecessor -1 and a cleanup action that adjusts ECX to one temporary then tail-jumps to 0x00891B80. The bank's COFF unwind map has the same predecessor and receiver displacement for every state. Each complete cleanup action was checked individually; build/008bf100-finish/checked-cleanup-000.log through checked-cleanup-177.log retain the raw checked-callee output. The detailed comparison is build/008bf100-finish/eh-comparison.json, and the retail map and action instructions are build/008bf100-finish/retail-eh.log. The emitted bank destructor is 22 bytes and has the same instructions after masking its one pool-global operand. This comparison does not waive the main-body mismatch or certify all DIR32 bindings.

The allocator call pushes one four-byte byte count, calls through VA 0x01337828, consumes a pointer in EAX and cleans the argument in the caller. Releases push one header pointer and call slot +4 of the table reached through VA 0x01337A30, again with caller cleanup. These are cdecl function-pointer calls, not virtual calls. The caller initializes the pool pointer to the allocation-pair address at 0x0089487A.

Both allocation-pair cells are in the PE's loader-zeroed interval. A raw file-offset lookup of those cells is invalid and was rejected using targets.load_target.read_rva; it is not evidence for an initial provider. The complete installer at 0x00789440 writes the allocation and free thunk addresses. The complete five-byte jumps at 0x007831E0 and 0x007831F0 reach operator new[] at 0x00881F70 (17 bytes) and operator delete[] at 0x00881EF0 (21 bytes), with no receiver or stack adjustment. The complete provider wrappers use one four-byte argument and plain RET, then forward to externally configured memory-manager callbacks with caller cleanup. Provider decoding and checks are retained in build/008bf100-finish/callback-audit.log and the corresponding decoded and checked logs. The external memory-manager callbacks were not identified in this retry.

## Verification and reopening condition

The scoped byte gate used build.verify_functions with one temporary selected row under the actual emitted symbol, without changing the ledger. It failed 1/1 on the saved body. Raw output is build/008bf100-finish/scoped-gate.log. check_csv.py and pin_consistency.py --check passed; their raw outputs are build/008bf100-finish/check-csv-final.log and build/008bf100-finish/pin-consistency-final.log. No full gate is required by this evidence-only change, and none was run.

The remaining blocker is codegen-order/EDI-save-and-EH-cleanup. The first-cleanup control-flow hypothesis is refuted for these two forms, not for every possible source structure. Reopening requires a distinct, supported explanation for the save placement that preserves the verified complete extent, all 178 temporary cleanup offsets and the callback ABI. A new body must improve the measured residue before replacing the unchanged bank, and full relocation and source-declaration acceptance checks remain necessary before any exact recovery claim.
