The tested base revision is `f392c3ed0bf9f6e8e9741ef2ffefdc0e6eadbbb7`. The production source is `game/GameEngine/Source/Common/Rva006ACD70VolumeTable.cpp`. It reproduces all 153 bytes and all three direct call targets at RVA `0x006ACD70`, with passing string, constant and DIR32 checks. Raw measurements and complete disassemblies are under `build/writer6-fin-bind/audio/`.

## Name and donor lineage

The saved reconstruction records Open BFME 2 donor `Code/GameEngineDevice/Source/MilesAudioDevice/MilesAudioManager.cpp` at game.dat RVA `0x0005910F`. That earlier donor audit is `006acd70-bfme2-donor.md`; the current finishing pack supplies the exact bank rather than another copy of the donor. This recovery independently checks BFME 1 instructions and declarations and retains the bank's names. It does not assert the donor's MilesAudioManager owner identity.

The existing `Rva006AE150Entry::adjust` name is contradicted by the ILT oracle, but `ilt_oracle.tier` classifies its address-derived owner as a placeholder. The finishing instructions explicitly permit retaining this name when an address-derived method would fail name preservation. An actual compiler experiment with `rva006ACD70` also emits 153 exact bytes, but `name_regression.regressions` reports `adjust -> rva006ACD70`. The production source therefore keeps `adjust`. Its bank-to-source comparison reports no regressions. Neither method name is claimed as an EA identity. No new callable pin is required.
