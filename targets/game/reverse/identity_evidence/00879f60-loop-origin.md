An origin of 1 changed only three displacement bytes, demonstrating that this lever reaches the residue without changing the frame or wider body. Origin 3 produced four differences and was rejected. The hypothesis would have been refuted if the changed induction origin retained the same displacement pair or changed surrounding instructions instead of reaching the target sequence.
