## Experiments and verification

The original bank reproduces 248 bytes with 43 non-relocation differences. Every difference is visible in build/2265f0-retry/bank-probe.log. The prologue assigns the rider to ESI and the receiver to EDI instead of retail's inverse assignment; that register difference propagates through the body. A separate load/store and address-computation sequence differs from +0xAE through +0xCB. In-class accessors were tested individually. The native unsigned-short `Player::getPlayerMask()` accessor restores the entire saved-register assignment and leaves 17 differing bytes confined to that pair sequence; the other individual accessors do not improve the bank. Its raw output is build/2265f0-retry/player_mask_accessor.log.
