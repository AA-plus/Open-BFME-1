The last group changes the builder-ID register and its push relative to the GameLogic load. That last scheduling change also moves the DIR32 operand by one byte, so the masked diagnostic score alone was not acceptance evidence.

The new hypothesis was that the earlier CastleMember lookup's direct member argument changed the compiler's scratch-allocation state. The refutation condition was an unchanged residue after copying that argument to a local. Introducing `Object *moduleObject = m_object;` immediately before the lookup removed every difference, including both call-order differences. The local preserves the original member reload; substituting the earlier `object` local would assert stability across the two virtual health calls that retail does not assert. The full extent is 339 bytes.
