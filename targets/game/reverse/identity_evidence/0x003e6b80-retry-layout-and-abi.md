## Two discrepancies in the existing evidence

The bank's AI::pathfinder accessor at +0x14 identifies the wrong owner. The complete AI constructor at 0x0014C170 stores the TAiData constructor result at AI+0x14 and the Pathfinder constructor result at AI+0x0C. AI::parseAiDataDefinition at 0x0014E580 loads AI+0x14 and passes it to INI::initFromINI together with the FieldParse table at VA 0x01094B00. Retail record VA 0x01094DD0 contains the string FormationColumnWidth, parser VA 0x00C52B20, a zero user-data word and offset 0xA0. The complete parser writes one float at the supplied destination. Thus the target's width read is a TAiData scalar field, not a Pathfinder field. The constructor also writes the field at +0xA0. The table and complete supporting decodes are retained as ai-field-table.json, ai-ctor.disasm, ai-data-ctor.disasm, ai-parse.disasm, ini-fields.disasm and ini-float.disasm, with their checked inventories. A different pointer assignment in the AI constructor or a different owner/offset in the table would refute this conclusion. Both checked facts agree.

The caller ledger prototype at 0x003F2160 also conflicts with its decoded argument use. With entry ESP denoted S, its normal saved frame is S-0x74. The initial cdecl copy helper receives five pushed stack slots, uses the first three and returns without stack cleanup. Its loop copies every field at offsets 0, 4 and 8 and advances both pointers by 12; its second return handles a nonpositive element count. After add esp,0x14, the first incoming argument is at [esp+0x78], the second at [esp+0x7C], the third at [esp+0x80] and the fourth at [esp+0x84]. The first argument becomes ECX for the call at caller+0x37E through ILT 0x00010EA1. That complete callee reads a pointer at receiver+0x23C, which is inconsistent with a three-float Coord3D receiver. The call to this target at caller+0x7DC pushes the saved diameter, zero, the goal cell and the second incoming argument, with the Pathfinder receiver in ECX. The caller's existing two-coordinate/int/bool prototype therefore cannot be treated as independent ABI proof. Its method name and the Zero Hour algorithm remain identity leads. Refutation would require a decoded stack adjustment that invalidates this argument mapping or a different receiver source at +0x37E. The complete caller, copy helper, receiver callee and target thunk are retained in caller.disasm, caller-copy.disasm, object-receiver.disasm and caller-thunk.disasm.

No descriptive bank names, pins, caller rows or neighbouring sources were changed. Trials using the corrected TAiData pointer type produce the unchanged target instruction bytes. These ownership findings require correction before presenting the bank as an evidence-complete recovery.
