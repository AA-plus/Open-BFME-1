Repeating register spelling, byte versus integer booleans, or the exhausted helper-visibility variants is not a justified next experiment. Complete boundary and direct-call ABI evidence were obtained; the remaining byte mismatch is a compiler-shape blocker, not evidence of an incomplete target.
