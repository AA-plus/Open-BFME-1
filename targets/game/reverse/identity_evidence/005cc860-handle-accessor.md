# Transfer body at RVA 0x005CC860

The retained `Rva005CC860Particle::seed(BfmeSeedTarget *)` reconstruction matches the complete 220-byte retail body. Its names remain those of the saved bank. The tested base revision is `a9a9595a201d1f9bf47cd0eef299dff205ec1120`, with model `gpt-6.1-sol`. Raw measurements and every trial source are retained under `build/5cc860_finish/`.

## Hypothesis and measurement

The new hypothesis was that the remaining redundant null guard comes from separately expanded handle and value accessors, rather than an inherently inaccessible register choice. A native handle followed by an in-class value getter preserves the EAX-to-EDI copy and second test. A null-first conditional in the arrow accessor preserves the fallback as the fall-through path. An unchanged guard-folded tail, a changed field offset, or a different helper ABI would refute this reconstruction.

The original bank emits 211 bytes. It has 37 differing emitted bytes and nine missing bytes, for 46 wrong bytes when missing output counts as wrong. The complete original probe is `build/5cc860_finish/baseline.log`; exact offset and relocation records are in `build/5cc860_finish/baseline.json`. Its differences occur at +0x14, +0xA4, +0xA6 through +0xAC, +0xB1 through +0xB3, +0xB6 through +0xC5, and +0xCA through +0xDB. The +0x14 and +0xA4 differences are branch displacements caused by the shorter tail. Starting at +0xA5, the bank reloads its volatile field instead of copying EAX to EDI. It then merges the two +0xAC loads, omits the second EDI-to-EAX copy, and shifts the final transfer and epilogue.

The typed getter with a positive pointer conditional emits 218 bytes and has 43 wrong bytes after restoring the separate slot at receiver +0x88 (`build/5cc860_finish/id_layout.log`). The null-first expression emits all 220 bytes with zero differing bytes (`build/5cc860_finish/id_null_first.log`). Correcting the four-byte ID to an enum, the +0x5C value to a complete 12-byte coordinate, and the virtual transfer return declarations preserves equality (`build/5cc860_finish/abi_typed.log`). The source at its final game path also matches (`build/5cc860_finish/final_source_probe.log`).

Other rejected trials change accessor definition sites, return a reference, introduce another accessor layer, use an explicit inline-only compiler setting, or make the boolean conversion out of line. Every source and unedited probe is retained separately, with measurements in `build/5cc860_finish/measurements.jsonl`. Some initial handle trials omitted the separate +0x88 slot; those trials are retained as invalid layout experiments and are not evidence for the final declaration.

The targeted copy-family generator was also run on the original bank (`build/5cc860_finish/family_copy.log`). Its only choices introduce notifier aliases in the already matching prefix. They do not reach the located handle-accessor residue and were excluded from further compiler search. The explicit accessor experiments above reach exact equality without changing that prefix.

## Fields, values and call bindings

The Particle constructor independently clears the three handle fields at receiver +0x7C, +0x80 and +0x84, then the separate +0x88 slot. The complete destructor calls ILT 0x00013994 on the +0x7C handle. That thunk reaches the complete 81-byte helper at RVA 0x001DA440. The helper reads owner, previous and next at offsets 0, 4 and 8, updates both neighbor links or owner +0x98 and +0x9C, and clears the previous and next fields. Both return paths were inspected. This proves the handle view independently of allocation size or a named pin; the present transfer only reads it and does not copy or change its ownership.

The actual ParticleSystem constructor at RVA 0x005CF850 reads its second four-byte stack argument and stores it at receiver +0xAC. The complete factory at RVA 0x005C33E0 passes the template, ID and boolean arguments to that constructor in right-to-left order. The complete null-system accessor at RVA 0x005CFF50 supplies ID -2 and returns the address of the system in EAX. The final view therefore represents +0xAC as a four-byte enum value returned in EAX, rather than the original bank's pointer. The ledger's named `getSystemID` helper at RVA 0x0049C950 reads +0x268 in its decoded body and was rejected as evidence for this layout. No unrelated row was changed.

The direct call routes are +0x31 through ILT 0x0002D27C to RVA 0x005BDC70, +0x6D through ILT 0x0000CAD1 to RVA 0x005C9740, +0xAB through ILT 0x00001B18 to RVA 0x005CFF50, and +0xCE through ILT 0x0000240A to RVA 0x0010C2E0. Complete decoded bodies and individual checked-callee logs establish the first two as ECX receivers with one callee-cleaned argument, the third as a no-argument pointer return, and the fourth as a two-argument cdecl forwarder. The final forwarder pushes a type table, the value address and width four to virtual slot +0x90. Existing pins for the retained null and forwarder spellings pass consistency checks in `build/5cc860_finish/pin_null.log` and `build/5cc860_finish/pin_forward.log`. No pin was added.

The transfer view follows the canonical Xfer slots: +0x10 returns a boolean in AL; +0x28 receives a two-byte version record; +0x60 receives a 12-byte coordinate; and +0x74 receives a four-byte unsigned value. The complete version, coordinate and unsigned overloads at RVAs 0x009D6430, 0x009D64F0 and 0x0006B2D0 validate those argument widths, callee cleanup and EAX reference returns. Their definitions and the canonical header were read. The three optional module calls use receiver pointers at +0x8C, +0x90 and +0x94, dispatch slot +0x0C with one stack argument, and retain opaque module identities. There is no STL container or constructor recovery in this change. No shared declaration was edited.
