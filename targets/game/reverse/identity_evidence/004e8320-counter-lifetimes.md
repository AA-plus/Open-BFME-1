## New hypothesis and measured improvement

The previous bank stages the building, money and unit results but gives the first built-count getter and the destroyed-building sum different scalar identities. Retail reuses EDX for these disjoint phases. The new hypothesis was that retaining one scalar identity across those phases would recover the first block without changing its arithmetic, field accesses or actual helper call.
