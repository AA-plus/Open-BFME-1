# Narrow money output reconstruction

The target remains a partial reconstruction. Open BFME 2 donor `Code/Libraries/Source/WWVegas/WWLib/stlport_narrow_money_put_string.cpp` at game.dat RVA `0x0000C410` is a near twin, not a verbatim donor. Its source supplies selective output-iterator inlining and a format local with eight-byte alignment. BFME 1 requires node allocation instead of the donor's malloc allocation, external conditional string copies, and a range constructor that calls the three-argument iterator-category helper.

Retail RTTI at VA `0x012C7508` names narrow `money_put`. Vtable VA `0x0112E96C` places this target in slot 1 and the independently named long-double overload in slot 2. STLport's reverse overload ordering identifies slot 1 as the string overload. The complete decoded 2,708-byte body has two `ret 0x1c` paths, including its final epilogue at RVA `0x00838451`. Its output iterator occupies two argument words and returns through hidden storage. The facet virtual calls use the declaration's slots, including character widening at slot 6 and format returns through an explicit storage pointer at slots 8 and 9. These observations would be refuted by a different RTTI owner, overload ordering, return extent, or argument map.

The actual range helper reached through ILT RVA `0x0002A531` ends with `ret 0xc` at RVA `0x002D87BE`. It reads two character pointers, allocates their distance plus a terminator, copies that many bytes, writes a zero byte, and stores three buffer pointers. The copy constructor reached through ILT RVA `0x0001CB11` reads the source's first two pointer fields and initializes all three destination fields. Its actual range helper at RVA `0x004FA6F0` was also decoded through its `ret 0xc`. The destructor reached through ILT RVA `0x0001642D` reads the buffer and allocation-end fields, uses global delete above 128 bytes, and otherwise calls the node allocator. The range constructor's base constructor at RVA `0x002D8610` initializes the same three pointer fields through its allocator proxy. That proxy helper at RVA `0x002D8570` writes its second argument to the receiver's word, returns the receiver in EAX, and ends with `ret 8`. Allocation size alone was not used to assign these types.

The money facet constructor at RVA `0x00847EF0` and both getter pairs at RVAs `0x00847F60` and `0x00847F70`, and `0x00848000` and `0x00848010`, establish four-byte patterns at receiver offsets `0xc` and `0x10`. This refutes an eight-byte pattern type. The aligned source local reproduces the retail `0xc8` frame without changing that type. The complete supporting decodes and checked callee inventories are retained in `build/money-donor-run/money-supporting-helpers-final.txt` and `build/money-donor-run/money-supporting-callees-final.txt`.
