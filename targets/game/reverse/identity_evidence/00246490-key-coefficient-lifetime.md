## Remaining differences and rejected experiments

The remaining offsets have three explanations. The branch byte at +0x163 and differing bytes from +0x16F through +0x1AA come from reciprocal lifetime, normalization join shape, coefficient materialization and the interleaved final-position copy. Retail preserves the reciprocal through the y and z multiplies and discards it at a common join; the candidate consumes it during z multiplication and uses an extra jump around the zero-path discard. The five register bytes at +0x1C3, +0x1C7, +0x1D7, +0x1E0 and +0x1E4 come from retail copying the coordinate destination from ECX to EAX before the three raw word stores. The differing bytes from +0x202 through +0x218 come from the final owner and Object-ID load order, scratch registers and delayed ID store. Exact individual offsets are retained in the fully bound log above. Side-by-side instructions are build/worker-00246490/083-key-coefficient-union-probe.log and build/worker-00246490/residue-union.log.

Trials covered constructor and noinline declarations, canonical Object and ObjectID views, helper visibility for map and both random functions, reference and value argument lifetimes, iterator conversion and increment temporaries, scalar and coordinate grouping, value-returning coordinate helpers, scalar precision, literal types, component operation order, delayed output stores, paired setters, reference getters, complete inline member helpers and loop-tail structure. Each retained source has its unedited probe output beside it under build/worker-00246490/. The running hypothesis and measurement list is build/verdict.txt. None improves the union candidate. The native finite store-family search also rejects reversing the delay and position stores; its raw outputs are build/worker-00246490/shape-store-search.log and build/worker-00246490/shape-store-001-probe.log. This is a measured plateau for those experiments, not a proof that all C++ forms or compiler scheduling are exhausted.
