## Extent and incoming ABI

The complete retail decode has returns at offsets +0x148 and +0x162. Every conditional branch remains inside the body, there is no external tail jump, and INT3 padding begins at +0x163. RVA 0x00006E83 is an instruction-aligned five-byte JMP to 0x004B0850. The complete 49-byte selector at 0x004B17E0 preserves its incoming ECX and tail-jumps to that thunk when the value copied into receiver +0x18 is nonzero. The complete callers at 0x004B1C80 and 0x004B0D40 also invoke this thunk with the same receiver and no stack arguments. The target returns with plain RET and has no hidden return storage. It changes receiver +0x20 and +0x24 from screen X and Y, and writes byte +0x08 when their Manhattan displacement exceeds two. Raw complete decodes and checked inventories are in build/finish-004b0850/retail-decoded.log, build/finish-004b0850/abi-decoded.log, build/finish-004b0850/checked-target.log and the checked-caller logs in the same directory. These callers establish the route and ABI, not the placeholder's original spelling.

## Compiler experiments and refutation

The new hypothesis was that aggregate scope and lvalue lifetime, rather than a missing helper or unproved ABI, caused the bank's scheduling differences. The saved bank was reproduced at 355 bytes with 58 differing non-relocation bytes, first at +0x4D. The hypothesis would have been refuted if scope and access changes preserved those decoded sequences. Giving the coordinate frame an inner lifetime changed the fallback copy from interleaved loads and stores to retail's three loads before its three stores. Accessing that frame's fields directly removed the extra x87 load and register-stack pop at the height merge. Reading the contact Z through a const volatile float pointer reproduced retail's float32 spill and reload. A pointer view of the existing stored screen pair recovered the Y-first absolute-value order. The resulting final source is exact at its intended game path.

The rejected trials include by-value accessors, memcpy and raw aggregate copies, scalar aggregate initialization, whole-frame and separate-local scope variations, neighbour compile flags and /Op, scalar and pointer-selected height merges, inline height and sum helpers, precision bit copies, absolute-value source permutations and helper forms, screen copies and references, and member union and array views. Their source snapshots and unedited probe outputs remain in build/finish-004b0850. build/verdict.txt records each measured trial. The family generator's copy and register scans supplied no applicable transformation for the located residue; its store choices only changed unrelated stores, so those choices were not searched. No assembly or generator dump was edited.
