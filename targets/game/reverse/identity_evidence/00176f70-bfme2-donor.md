# Melee-entry donor experiment

The tested base revision is `5345c7c3e1d23869406c66dc68b5774a110da8ee`. The donor is Open BFME 2 `Code/GameEngine/Source/GameLogic/AI/AIAttackMeleeApproachStateOnEnter.cpp`, function `onEnter` at game.dat RVA `0x0034D5B7`. Its 719-byte sequel body is a same-name variant, not a verbatim BFME 1 body. The existing BFME 1 bank's descriptive callees, members and method identity were retained in every trial. The donor's different member offsets, extra range arguments and extra victim predicate were not imported into BFME 1.

## Evidence and refutation test

The current ledger still owns RVA `0x00176F70` through a 747-byte generated dump. The complete target decodes through its final plain RET at `+0x2EA`; all nine return paths and direct branches remain inside this extent. The checked-callees inventory passes and retains the actual ILT routes. The independently installed vtable `0x0109A6C0` reaches the target through slot 4 and ILT `0x00011667`; matched construction and destruction witnesses are described in `00176f70.md`. The ILT oracle confirms the bank's existing public virtual decoration.

The complete actual length helper at RVA `0x000B6CA0` reads floats at offsets zero, four and eight, squares all three, adds them and returns their square root in ST0 with plain RET. The target zeroes the third component and passes the local receiver in ECX. The actual weapon helper at RVA `0x001E8930` consumes source, victim and one extra four-byte word, forwards their position prefixes and the extra bits to its helper, returns AL and uses RET12. BFME 2's four-stack-word weapon call is therefore not the BFME 1 ABI. The bank's three-word declaration remains correct. Neither this target nor its distance locals has an exception frame or owning container cleanup.

The new hypothesis was that the donor's shared inline distance helper, which copies all three position fields, subtracts the saved position, takes XY snapshots and constructs a zero-Z vector, changes source-pointer liveness enough to reproduce the second AI-data load in ESI. A second evidence change is the current canonical `Coord3D` header, which is a 12-byte aggregate rather than the older class with user-declared constructors and destructor used by the bank. These address native value lifetime and declaration context rather than another commutation of the existing three differing instructions. A same or worse measured result refutes each tested port.

## Measurements and retained body

The original bank reproduces 747 bytes, 32 aligned relocations and three differing non-relocation operand bytes. The first differing instruction begins at `+0x1E6`: retail loads AI data into ESI while the bank uses EDI. The two affected x87 operand bases begin at `+0x205` and `+0x20B`. The actual differing bytes lie one byte into each instruction. The ordinary resolved candidate gate fails exactly this body and reproduces the same register substitutions. Its complete raw target and resolved compiled streams are retained.

The complete donor distance helper with the bank's coordinate class emits 589 bytes and 461 differences within the common emitted extent; it also omits 158 requested bytes. The helper with canonical aggregate coordinates emits 766 bytes and 346 non-relocation differences. Applying canonical aggregate coordinates directly to the saved bank's distance blocks emits 800 bytes and 591 differences. Applying the donor's `/O1 /G7 /arch:SSE` flags to its canonical helper port emits 684 bytes and 539 differences within the common extent; it also omits 63 requested bytes. None improves the preferred bank. The shorter results are failures over the full requested extent, not higher scores.

The required family generator, run on both the original bank and the canonical donor helper, offers only swapping `m_adjustDestinations=false` and `field50=0`. That option is the already rejected store-order experiment in the pre-dispatch history, and the existing order agrees with retail at `+0xA0` and `+0xA4`, so it was not compiled again. No unsupported scalar assembly, new pin or production body was introduced. Further reopening needs a grounded declaration or lifetime change that frees ESI at `+0x1E6` without delaying the load or changing the x87 sequence. An unused budget is not that evidence.

The preferred body remains unchanged at `targets/game/reverse/attempts/0x00176f70.cpp`. Its measured non-relocation difference ratio is `1 - 3 / 747 = 0.9959839357`; the inherited author label remains `0.996`.
