## Experiment and refutation

The saved bank reproduced at 357 bytes with 14 differences. Its listing has `pos` at -28 and `sphere` at -16, with a 0x1C frame. Retail uses a 0x28 frame and places X and Y at the original coordinate's slots while placing Z in a second vector slot. The exact native spelling first copies the returned coordinate through Vector3's copy constructor, then constructs the sphere with `Vector3(pos.X, pos.Y, pos.Z)`. The listing names `pos` at -40, compiler temporary `$T4092` at -28, and `sphere` at -16. Scalar optimization keeps the original X and Y values but stores Z in `$T4092`, accounting for retail's split component homes without synthetic padding or assembly. The three objects supply the complete frame.

Every original differing byte is located in build/00431a60-writer4/bank-offsets.log. The prologue and epilogue immediates account for the frame size. Three references use the relocated Z home, three use the relocated sphere-radius home, and the remaining six refer to incoming arguments whose offsets rise with the frame. No residual field offset, instruction order, constant value, or branch destination differs in the recovered body.

The new hypothesis was that preserving both the native vector copy and the scalar constructor's temporary would recover that allocation. It would be refuted by the same 14 offsets, extra stores, a changed instruction sequence, or a relocation target disagreement. Native copy combined with pointer construction, direct sphere construction, unary plus, address construction, or assignment retained the original residue. Native copy combined with the three-component construction was exact. The individual scalar-constructor lever recorded in earlier history was insufficient because those trials retained the POD Coord3D local.

Debug information switches /Z7 and /Zi, disabling intrinsics, and disabling string pooling left the original residue. Disabling global optimization, retaining the frame pointer, and optimizing for size changed instruction structure and were rejected. These compiler-context tests did not justify adding an otherwise unobserved semantic local.
