## Hypothesis and measurements

The preferred bank was reproduced before editing. It emits 354 bytes against the complete 358-byte retail extent, with 29 differing overlap bytes and four missing bytes. Its first differing arithmetic instruction is at +0x123. Retail loads position X before adding displacement X, loads position Y before adding the live random-adjusted Y in ST1, discards the extra x87 stack value, and loads position Z before adding displacement Z. The earlier branch displacement at +0x9C and later setter relocation and epilogue differences follow from this four-byte instruction-length difference. The unedited output is build/finish-002bd940/00-bank.probe.log; every differing byte offset and the four omitted tail bytes are enumerated in bank-residue.log.

The first new hypothesis was that a scalar lifetime or native coordinate setter would retain retail's Y temporary. The sources and raw outputs are build/finish-002bd940/01-y-scalar-lifetime.cpp through 04-native-setter-two.cpp and their corresponding .probe.log files. The next hypothesis was that explicit ordered position reads would reproduce the observed x87 operand order. It would be refuted by a remaining instruction difference or an additional access. Reading each position operand through a `const volatile float` view emits exactly 358 bytes modulo relocation slots, including the original access sequence. The final source retains only the explicit reads and ordinary stores.
