## Compiler experiment and verification

The saved bank emitted 252 bytes against retail's 240. Probe reported 48 non-relocation differences starting at +0xAE, with another 12 excess bytes (build/006ee3f0/00-bank-probe.log). The differing instructions are entirely in the descriptor load, loop and shifted epilogue. Retail keeps the descriptor receiver in EAX; the bank reloads its W3DDisplay member twice per iteration and assigns the loop index to EAX.

Removing the bank barrier worsened the result. A bool flag, typed pointer subtraction, const descriptor binding, word-sized opaque padding and /Ob1 did not close it. Native vector accessors produced a longer body. A visible reset helper fixed the descriptor binding but shared its initial begin load with element access, leaving a different 241-byte loop. A loop-body compiler barrier prevents that sharing and reproduces the complete retail sequence. Numbered trial snapshots, their unedited probe outputs, the final source snapshot and the strict gate output remain in build/006ee3f0/. The final probe is build/006ee3f0/final-probe-pinned.log. The source preserves the displayed, Count, descriptor, product and font names already present in the bank.

The strict scoped gate passes with final body pins (build/006ee3f0/scoped-gate.log), including relocation resolution, DIR32, literal and boundary checks. Pin consistency, canonical class adoption, declared-function coverage and the name oracle pass in build/006ee3f0/pin-consistency.log, build/006ee3f0/class-gate-final.log, build/006ee3f0/final-declared-unmatched.log and build/006ee3f0/name-oracle-final.log. Its underlying bank-to-source comparison passes with no findings (build/006ee3f0/final-name-checks.log). Oracle checks contradict the original spellings of the address-derived target and product views; their placeholder tiers are retained and exempt from claiming a real name.
