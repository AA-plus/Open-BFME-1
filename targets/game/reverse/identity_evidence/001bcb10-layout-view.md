The naming witness associates Locomotor+0x3c with m_preferredHeight. The inherited bank calls that word m_donutTimer. The complete neighboring constructor at 0x001B60E0 zeroes +0x3c, and copies the resolved template word at +0x54 into Locomotor+0x44. The target writes only a four-byte zero at +0x3c. The alleged preferred-height getter at 0x001B46A0 loads float [ECX+0x3c], but its current lexical identity is contradicted by the ILT oracle API. These observations challenge the shared witness without proving the lexical identity of the word at +0x3c. Raw name-oracle.log, preferred-height-witness.log, decode-001b60e0.log, decode-001b46a0.log, checked-001b60e0.log and boundary.log preserve the conflict.

The recovered source keeps the inherited m_donutTimer identifier inside the address-derived view. It does not rename that field or assert a corrected canonical Locomotor layout. The Locomotor declaration retains only the original leading m_vtable word and the existing method declarations. No object is constructed through either local declaration. The independent physical widths and offsets are sufficient for this body; the canonical field's lexical correction remains separate work. This view would be refuted by an independently identified caller receiver adjustment or a decoded store at a different offset.
