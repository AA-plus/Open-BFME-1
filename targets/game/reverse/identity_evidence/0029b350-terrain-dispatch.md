# PhysicsBehavior dispatch at 0x0029B350

The complete 315-byte body is recovered as `PhysicsBehavior::rva0029B350(bool)`. Its original method name is unknown. The existing bank's descriptive identifiers and existing callee declarations remain in use. This recovery was tested against revision `50d4d6d59a69d4ec8504ef89e6d0223ccea0f163` with MSVC 7.1. No new pin or shared header is required.

## Retry and byte evidence

The saved bank was measured independently before editing it. It emits 315 bytes with 59 non-relocation differences, starting at `+0xB9`. The complete instruction comparison is retained in build/b350-sol/baseline-probe.log, and the unchanged bank is preserved in build/b350-sol/baseline.cpp. All earlier verdicts were read from build/b350-sol/history.log. Their scope, local lifetime, expression-order and compiler-option experiments were not repeated.

Retail loads the terrain receiver at `+0xB0`, its vtable into EDX at `+0xB9`, and pushes the null normal pointer at `+0xBE`. The bank pushes null at `+0xB9` but delays the vtable load until `+0xF5`. This transposes the intervening coordinate instructions by two bytes through `+0xF5`; the bank also discards the remaining x87 value before the X argument load, while retail loads X first at `+0xF8` and discards the x87 value at `+0xFC`. The indirect call at `+0xFF` and everything from `+0x102` onward already match. These instruction movements account for the remaining differences rather than a wrong coordinate offset or arithmetic operation. build/b350-sol/analysis-summary-final.log retains the exact differing byte offsets.

The new hypothesis was that a typed capture of the actual terrain dispatch table before the address-exposed coordinate stores would recover retail's schedule. It would be refuted by an extra receiver instruction, a changed stack contract, a remaining scheduling difference, or a strict binding failure. Both capture placements, before the delta and before the position, emit the exact 315-byte instruction stream. Raw outputs are build/b350-sol/table-before-delta-probe.log and build/b350-sol/table-before-pos-probe.log. A coordinate union and aggregate assignment instead emit 336 bytes with 117 differences; this declaration-based alternative is rejected. Its source and output are build/b350-sol/coord-union.cpp and build/b350-sol/coord-union-probe.log.

The final source forward-declares the canonical `TerrainLogic` owner instead of privately redefining it. Its table entry uses a const receiver and the canonical `Coord3D *` normal argument. The register-only second argument carries the table itself in EDX. ECX still carries the unadjusted receiver, all three real arguments remain on the stack, and the result remains in ST0. This is the proven x86 thiscall call shape expressed through a fastcall pointer, following the adapter described in `docs/shape_levers.md`. It adds no stack argument. build/b350-sol/canonical-terrain-contract-probe.log and build/b350-sol/final-source-probe.log retain exact probes of the ABI refinement and the final source.

The original method's descriptive name remains unproven and is deliberately not claimed.
