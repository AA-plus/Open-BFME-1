The saved bank already reproduces the complete constructor's instruction shape. Its strict residue consists exclusively of five call operands, rather than register allocation, local placement or exception-region structure. `strict01-bank.log` and `trial01-bank-metrics.json` record every differing byte: +0x30 through +0x33, +0x41 through +0x43, +0xA0 through +0xA3, +0xE7 through +0xEA, and +0x106 through +0x109. The new hypothesis is that a translation-unit-scoped name for the native STLport vector base preserves its implementation while making its binding specific to this retail copy. The inspected `VectorVoidPtrCopyConstructor.cpp` and `LargeGroupAudioKeyMapCopyConstructor.cpp` use that exact mechanism for other distinct retail base copies. The experiment defines `_Vector_base` as `Rva001F9330VectorBase` only while including `<vector>` and then undefines it. It does not change the native vector algorithms or the existing shared base pin.

`probe02-scoped-base.log` preserves the masked result. `strict03-bound.log` and `strict04-final.log` show that the scoped candidate passes the repository's strict `build.verify_functions` comparison with no unresolved calls. `helper-shapes03-bound.log` independently compares the complete emitted helper bodies with resolved call targets, rather than masking their calls.
