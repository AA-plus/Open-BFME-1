## Hypothesis and result

Refutation would be a complete measured body no closer than the saved bank, with missing bytes counted as wrong. The current headers alone reproduce the saved bank's result. These changes improve the body and eliminate the switch, flag, pair and iterator displacement differences. They do not explain retail's additional unused frame region.

## Rejected shapes and remaining blockers

An explicit operation local under `/Od /Ob0` improves to 338 bytes with 65 differences but keeps comparisons with case literals. Named end iterators emit 344 bytes with 205 differences, or 204 with `/Og`, because they add address reloads. Scalar value initialization, scalar braces, a char flag and a named one-byte receiver aggregate do not improve the 338-byte shape. Copying a value-initialized receiver into a named local adds a byte copy and emits 345 bytes. These sources and raw results remain in the task folder.

With the temporary receiver, leaving the pointer in the outer scope gives 339 bytes with 23 differences. Limiting the pair's lifetime improves this to eight. Keeping the pointer and pair together in the inner block instead gives 22 differences. The prescribed family generator produces no applicable choices for either the snapshot candidate or the retained candidate. No fake frame padding, assembly call plumbing or new pin was added. The two inherited marker-only assembly brackets remain diagnostic; their symbolic self-references reproduce the decoded marker operands without image-address literals.

The remaining source blocker is `stack-slot/reserved-frame-tail`: retail reserves a further 28 bytes before the receiver and switch temporaries, without any decoded access to that region. The evidence does not establish what original declaration or compiler setting caused it. Reopening needs independent evidence for that allocation, or a justified compiler-lifetime experiment; another unchanged register spelling does not address it.
