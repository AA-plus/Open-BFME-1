# RVA 0x005ACD40 attack command

The target is a complete 456-byte thiscall body at RVA `0x005ACD40`, tested against base revision `35d43826b6f498b622ee009393719cd0154fee33`. It is recovered as `Rva005ACD40CommandTranslator::issueAttackCommand`. The address remains in the owner because the exact retail decoration is unresolved. EA's strongly paired `CommandTranslator::issueAttackCommand` label and the Zero Hour body establish the attack-command role, but neither proves the additional BFME parameter's enum identity or the original access specifier. No new pin is introduced.

## Retry and measurements

The five ledger attempt records have no banked body. Their latest reported experiment emitted 467 bytes against 456, with 305 differing bytes and a receiver/frame blocker. The new evidence relative to those records is the landed `Rva0041A2E0::flash(const RGBColor *)` body, its caller and unwind evidence, and the canonical Object header's `m_containedBy` field at `+0x214`. The proposed experiment uses that native field, the virtual `Object::getDrawable() const` at slot 10, and the complete native 12-byte location local. An unchanged or worse receiver/frame shape would refute the retry hypothesis.

The first complete candidate emitted exactly 456 bytes with zero differences outside 13 relocation slots. Its source is `build/attack-005ACD40/trial01.cpp`; the direct subprocess reproduction in `build/attack-005ACD40/probe01-raw.log` preserves raw stdout and stderr without PowerShell formatting. The initial PowerShell output remains in `build/attack-005ACD40/probe01.log`. The final source at `game/GameEngine/Source/GameClient/MessageStream/Rva005ACD40AttackCommand.cpp` also emitted exactly 456 bytes, with raw output in `build/attack-005ACD40/probe-final.log`. Adopting the existing GameMessage header preserves the exact result in `build/attack-005ACD40/probe-header-final.log`. The first candidate's invented nested GUI enum is not retained. The fourth parameter is a 32-bit integer, and the owner retains the address after four tested real-name decorations were contradicted by the actual ILT target slot. `build/attack-005ACD40/identity-audit2.log` records the tests through the Python oracle API, without the command-line address ambiguity. Byte equality is separate from identity evidence.

The probe history subsequently exposed older unbanked scratch that was absent from the attempt records: `build/005ACD40/trial01.cpp` and `build/005ACD40/trial03.cpp`. Both were read and measured again without editing them. The former emits 456 bytes with four operand differences at `+0x122`, `+0x126`, `+0x128` and `+0x12A`, all in the selected-drawable call setup. The latter is already exact after relocation masking. Raw results are `build/attack-005ACD40/probe-prior01.log` and `build/attack-005ACD40/probe-prior03.log`. Its actual public CommandTranslator decoration with an integer fourth parameter is also contradicted by the target's ILT slot, independently checked in `build/attack-005ACD40/final-audit2.log`. The conversion therefore completes independently verified identity and ABI work with an address-qualified owner; it claims no new compiler-shape improvement over that unbanked exact trial. The existing scratch and logs remain intact.

## Boundary and incoming ABI

`build/attack-005ACD40/target-decode.log` contains the complete target and following INT3 padding. The four returns at `+0x1E`, `+0x38`, `+0x58` and `+0x1C5` all clean 16 argument bytes. The last return ends at `+0x1C8`, exactly the ledger extent. Every conditional branch and unconditional jump stays within the body. There is no tail transfer, hidden return storage or EH registration. EAX returns the 32-bit message type. ECX supplies the receiver whose byte at `+8` is the selected-team flag, as independently used by the landed move command at `0x005ACBB0`.

The raw call-index hit at RVA `0x005AF151` is verified on an instruction boundary inside the complete caller at `0x005ADE90`. Its code ends with `ret 12` at `0x005AF86D`; the following bytes at `0x005AF870` are jump-table data. `build/attack-005ACD40/select-and-caller-complete.log` contains all 6624 code bytes, and `build/attack-005ACD40/callees-caller.log` is the checked inventory. Its two byte-indexed switch tables at `0x005AF870` and `0x005AF8C4` have 12 and 10 entries; both 36-byte selectors and every entry are checked against complete decoded instruction boundaries in `build/attack-005ACD40/extent-and-table-audit.log`. At the call, ECX is reloaded from the caller's saved receiver at `[esp+0x1C]`. Four pushes pass Drawable, location pointer, command-evaluation dword and zero, in that order after reversing stack push order. ILT `0x00037AD3` jumps directly to this target. The caller stores the EAX result as its message type. The location argument is a pointer to a three-component value, used by the target and its append-location callee at offsets 0, 4 and 8. The fourth argument is not assigned an unproven enum identity.

## Indirect calls and selection payload

The target uses no receiver adjustment.

Object's primary vtable at VA `0x0109EE58` routes slot 10 (`+0x28`) through ILT `0x0002074D` to the complete seven-byte getter at `0x001BE440`, returning Object `+0x80` in EAX. InGameUI's installed vtable at VA `0x010F5B38` routes slot 63 (`+0xFC`) through ILT `0x000377C7` to the complete four-byte getter at `0x0043B250`. It returns the address of the list at receiver `+0x18`. `build/attack-005ACD40/vtable-ui-correct.log` and `build/attack-005ACD40/virtual-decode.log` retain this route. Several older ledger notes use `0x00CF5B38`, which is the table's RVA, as though it were its VA; the initial lookup at that VA found no slots and is not used as evidence.

The complete 350-byte `InGameUI::selectDrawable` body at `0x004462D0` independently establishes list construction. Its incoming Drawable pointer is retained in EBP, tested through Drawable's selected byte at `+0x3AC`, and used as the receiver of the selection helper. At `+0xFD`, its inline value construction writes exactly that one dword to destination-node `+8`, with no other payload access. There is no key or pair payload. `build/attack-005ACD40/select-and-caller-complete.log` and `build/attack-005ACD40/callees-select.log` retain all paths, including its `ret 4` and full inline value construction.

STLport 4.6's `inputs/vendor/stlport/stl/_list.h` declares two link pointers in `_List_node_base`, one `_Tp` value in `_List_node`, and a one-pointer iterator. The complete value-construction evidence above, the getter route and consumption as a Drawable receiver support `_STL::list<Drawable *>` independently of a donor or STL pin. This conversion adds no STL-named pin or separately claimed template body.

## Refutation and acceptance

An outgoing target branch, a caller passing a different argument representation, an indirect route with another receiver adjustment, a value-copy field beyond the single pointer, or a strict relocation failure would refute this recovery. The EA and Zero Hour names support semantics; the source's address token explicitly retains uncertainty about the original decoration. Existing Coord3D, Thing, Object and GameMessage headers are included. The StatsCollector member at `+0x14` is witnessed as `m_attackCommands` by `name_oracle.py`, with raw output in `build/attack-005ACD40/name-stats.log`. No shared header, policy, tooling or existing pin is edited.

`build/attack-005ACD40/add-match.log` records the verified scaffold replacement, including strict byte comparison, relocation resolution, DIR32 validation and body guard. `build/attack-005ACD40/scoped-gate-final.log` is the independent final-source gate. Passing declaration, class, name and pin checks are recorded in `declared-unmatched-final.log`, `class-gate-final.log`, `name-check-final.log` and `pin-consistency.log` under the same directory. A full gate is not required because no header or shim is changed. The initial CSV check passed. The final CSV check reports exactly one problem: the new source exists but is absent from the read-only Git index. `build/attack-005ACD40/check-csv-final.log` retains the raw refusal. The coordinator must stage the source and ledger transaction before running the normal hooks; no Git write or checker bypass was used.
