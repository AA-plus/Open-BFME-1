## Remaining mismatch

The first-loop induction and branch order now match retail. Five differing operand bytes at +0x012, +0x072, +0x07D, +0x0CC and +0x0D7 all address minimum at ESP+0x10 instead of retail ESP+0x14. Retail subsequently reuses ESP+0x14 for the second angle and distance; both candidate and retail already agree on the later angle slots.

The first remaining structural divergence is at +0x305. Retail keeps the cosine product on x87, exchanges it with the angle, computes the sine product and spills that product at ESP+0x2C. The candidate instead spills the cosine product at ESP+0x28, computes sine without that exchange, and keeps the sine product for the updates. This changes the duplicate, load, subtraction and final FPU cleanup sequence through retail +0x349. The candidate success path shares a final FSTP with the skipped-offset path; retail consumes its retained cosine product earlier and jumps directly to the loop join. The resulting loop join and final copy are four bytes earlier in the candidate. The branch displacement differences at +0x15E, +0x17E and +0x2E4 follow this change. The remaining later byte differences, including the four missing final bytes, are consequences of this instruction sequence and its shifted tail. The complete offset list and both instruction streams are retained in the receipt and raw probe outputs listed below.

## Rejected hypotheses and reopening

The interrupted run's measured trials are retained in build/worker-runs/20261013-085447/verdict.txt and build/finish-0079d530/. They include floating-point consistency, exception and CPU flags, narrow and unsigned neighbor indices, declaration order, staged unit-pair scaling, distance lifetimes, visible subtraction, offset copies, reference versus explicit result storage, explicit fallback induction, scalar versus aggregate updates, offset constructors and minimum-only aggregate storage. The explicit first-loop fallback and scalar update trial provides the stronger bank. All earlier attempt rows were retrieved again in build/resume-0079d530/session-target.log.

The continuation tested a reference-selected minimum, pair storage for the two angles, a double length result, double cosine-product width, paired updates with value and reference arguments, Pentium 4 scheduling, an actual two-field workspace reused between minimum and angles, equivalent array storage, and extraction of the entire reduction into an inlined member. The reference minimum changes the first-loop operations; double length introduces FCOMPP rather than the witnessed FCOM; the wider product changes temporary offsets; workspace storage still enlarges the frame; and the remaining forms preserve the unresolved product residence. Sources, complete raw probes, emitted bytes and offset receipts are retained in build/resume-0079d530/. These observations do not prove that assembly is required.

A retry needs a new source-structure hypothesis that specifically produces a cosine product retained across FSIN while spilling the sine product, or a independently justified scalar lifetime that moves minimum to ESP+0x14 without enlarging the frame. Another unchanged register permutation, operand reorder or already tested polar helper is not a reopening condition. The preferred bank is a measured partial, not an exact source recovery.
