# Constructor recovery at 0x0061DA30

The reconstruction retains `Rva0061DA30Base` from the saved bank. This is an address-derived owner, not a proven EA class identity. The tested starting revision is `a1dbd9b99864bd5a06412fe4a354a9090d82e07c`. The saved bank reproduced 252 bytes with 61 differences outside relocation slots. Raw results are in build/da30/bank-probe.log, and each trial source and its unedited probe output remain in build/da30/.

## Boundary, argument and string ownership

The complete decoded body spans 0x0061DA30 through 0x0061DB2B inclusive. Its only return is `ret 4` at +0xF9, followed by INT3 padding. There are no conditional branches or tail jumps in the constructor. ECX is saved as the receiver in ESI. The constructor takes the address of its four-byte stack argument, copies it into the member at receiver +4 through 0x00887B60, releases the stack argument through 0x00887940, and returns the receiver in EAX. The verified ILT at 0x0001BD5B jumps directly to this body. Complete decoded callers at 0x0060BEB0, 0x0060BBD0, 0x0060AAE0, 0x0060A470 and 0x0060ACB0 construct that four-byte argument through the same string copy helper. The allocation and use of EAX in 0x0060A470 establish the constructor result independently of the bank's declaration. Raw decoding is in build/da30/decode.log; checked inventories are in build/da30/callees-target.log and build/da30/checked-*.log.

The entire 121-byte copy helper reads exactly the source's pointer at +0, writes it to the destination at +0, and increments the pointed-to header's reference count when non-null. The entire 134-byte release helper decrements that count, frees the buffer when it reaches zero, and clears the object's pointer. Their critical-section paths merge back into these operations and terminate at `ret 4` and `ret` respectively. No second object field is copied or destroyed. The source includes the canonical ascii_string.h and its StringBase declaration. These complete helper bodies, their ownership operations and the caller's argument construction justify the AsciiString member and by-value parameter. There is no STL container in this constructor.

## Exception cleanup

Retail's handler is at 0x00C3F0F8 and its FuncInfo is at 0x00E2EAC0. The sole unwind entry maps state 0 to -1 with action 0x00C3F0F0. That eight-byte action executes `lea ecx, [ebp+4]` and jumps through ILT 0x0000D828 to the AsciiString destructor at 0x0005EE90, which tail-jumps to the completely decoded release helper at 0x00887940. State 0 covers the parameter during member copying; the main body resets it to -1 before normal parameter destruction. The scalar stores cannot throw and introduce no additional ownership or cleanup. Raw retail metadata and cleanup instructions are in build/da30/eh-target.log and build/da30/slots-fixed.log. The final object's sole unwind state also maps 0 to -1, and its eight-byte cleanup has the same receiver adjustment and a relocation to the existing AsciiString destructor. This comparison is retained in build/da30/final-abi-eh.log.

## Vtable and layout view

The installed table is at VA 0x01117624. Its ten entries resolve through ILTs to 0x0061DB70, 0x0061DBF0, 0x0061DCA0, 0x0061CE00, 0x0061DF60, 0x0061E080, 0x0061E230, 0x0061D2D0, 0x0061D140 and 0x0061D150 in that order. Slot zero reaches the already-matched render-object creation body, not a destructor. The bank's virtual destructor declaration therefore does not describe the retail table. The final translation-unit view uses an explicit pointer member at +0 and the address-derived external `g_Va01117624`; it retains the bank's constructor, destructor and member names. The constructor makes no indirect or virtual call. The table is referenced at its decoded address, and no new method identity or virtual argument ABI is claimed. Raw entry bytes and decoded thunk destinations are in build/da30/slots-fixed.log.

The string member is at +4. Every scalar store in the complete constructor supplies its offset, width and initial bits independently of allocation size. The byte stores are at +0x4C, +0x6C and +0x7C. The dword fields initialized to integer 1 are at +0x2C, +0x38 and +0x40. The remaining dword fields retain the bank's offset-derived names and literal values. Their source-level semantic identities remain unknown. The view's qualifiers control ordered stores; they do not establish that EA declared these fields volatile.

## New hypothesis and compiler experiments

The initial hypothesis was that the residual instruction order came from declarations or visible scalar assignment helpers rather than an immutable register-allocation choice. It would have been refuted if these alternatives retained the same instruction sequence. Ordinary storage for the later byte fields removed misplaced flag stores and reduced the residue to 37 differences. Ordinary storage for the three one-valued fields reduced it to 26 differences, proving that the earlier all-volatile workaround concealed a declaration-sensitive schedule.

A static inline store helper taking a volatile int reference reproduces the witnessed order of the three one stores and the following six zero stores. The reference and pointer forms both reproduced all 252 bytes outside relocation slots. The helper adds no call, allocation, read or lifetime. Its name is address-derived, and its definition is marked absent-from-retail. An explicit ordinary vtable pointer in the initializer list also reproduced all 252 bytes; a volatile pointer left 10 differences and was rejected. Raw exact probes are in build/da30/access-helper-ref.log, build/da30/access-helper-pointer.log and build/da30/explicit-vptr.log.

Rejected experiments include CPU scheduling switches, size optimization, disabling global optimization, a frame-pointer change, the applicable EH generator combinations, byte-field signedness, selective float volatility, partial ordinary dword declarations, unsigned and enum one fields, partial member initializers, direct volatile reference casts, a volatile receiver view, explicit offset access and grouped setters. Per-trial results and refutation conditions are retained in build/verdict.txt. No assembly, new function pin, shared header change or baseline change was used.

The final source at game/GameEngine/Source/GameLogic/LivingWorld/Rva0061DA30BaseConstructor.cpp probes exact at the independently verified 252-byte extent. `add_match.py --replace-rva` passed its scoped gate, including the function bytes, source claims, DIR32 address check and body guard with no baselined findings. Its raw output is in build/da30/add-match.log. A separate final scoped build through build.sh also passed in build/da30/scoped-gate-bash.log. The tool repointed the dump row, wrote its tombstone and removed the obsolete bank. The complete original bank remains preserved in build/da30/bank.cpp.

The declared-unmatched check, class gate, pin consistency check and member-name oracle passed. The bank-to-source comparison through `name_regression.regressions` reports no regressions in build/da30/final-abi-eh.log; this revision's command-line name_regression accepts Git revisions rather than source paths. The constructor's invented address-derived name is contradicted by the ILT hash oracle, as expected for a placeholder. This does not supply an EA identity, and the address token remains. The inline store helper is absent from retail and is not pinned to the constructor or any other body. There are no newly claimed real function names.

The final `check_csv.py` run is blocked by collection state: it tries to read the deleted bank because the read-only Git index still lists that file, and raises FileNotFoundError. The exact raw traceback is retained in build/da30/check-csv-final.log. The initial clean-tree check passed in build/da30/check-csv-initial.log. The coordinator must stage the new source and evidence, the ledger and tombstone, and the bank deletion together, then rerun the normal checks. The conversion remains in the working tree; no Git write or synchronization was performed. No full gate is required by a shared-header or shim change because neither was edited.

The recovery would be refuted by a decoded caller that supplies a different-width argument, a different complete copy-helper field footprint, an unwind cleanup that owns a different object, a different installed table address, or failure of the scoped relocation-aware byte gate on the final source. The source-level EA owner and the scalar member meanings remain unproven and are not claimed.
